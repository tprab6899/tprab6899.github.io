Pilot version · Terms version 2026-11-24

PainMap is not a diagnosis or medical advice. For severe, sudden or worsening pain, see a health professional or call 811. In an emergency, call 911.

1. Who we are

PainMap is a pain tracking app made by Theodore Prabhu in Toronto, Ontario. "We" and "us" mean PainMap. You can reach us at painmapcanada@gmail.com.

2. Agreeing to these terms

By ticking "I understand" and using PainMap, you agree to these terms and to our privacy page. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

During the pilot, PainMap is only for adults 18 and over.

3. The first pilot phase is for clinicians only

In the first phase of the pilot, PainMap is only for health professionals testing it themselves, to check its content. Please don't use it with patients, or ask patients to use it, until we tell you in writing that the patient phase has started.

4. What PainMap does

PainMap helps you mark where it hurts on a 3D body, answer questions about your pain, keep a pain diary, and make a report to bring to a health professional. It also shows:

  • Possible causes: general ideas that match what you entered, to discuss with a doctor, nurse or physiotherapist. They are not a diagnosis, they may be wrong, and the right answer may not be on the list.
  • Recovery plans and exercise demos: general examples of gentle exercises. They are not a treatment plan made for you. Don't start, change or stop any exercise or treatment without checking with your own health professional, and stop if anything hurts more.
  • Warning signs: some symptoms that need care quickly, with when to call 911 or 811. This is not a complete list. Not seeing a warning doesn't mean you're fine.

5. What PainMap doesn't do

  • It doesn't diagnose any condition or replace an assessment by a health professional.
  • It doesn't watch your entries or alert anyone if something is wrong.
  • The medical content hasn't been fully reviewed by a licensed clinician yet. Checking it is the reason for the first pilot phase. The French medical text is a draft.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Always follow your own health professional over anything in the app.
  • Don't delay getting care because of something you saw, or didn't see, in PainMap.
  • You decide what you enter and who you show your report to.
  • Don't misuse the app, try to break it, or copy it to make your own product.

7. This is a pilot version

PainMap is still being tested. It may have mistakes or bugs, change without notice, or stop working. Your entries are saved only on your phone (see the privacy page), so they can be lost if you clear your browser or switch phones. Use Settings → Back up my data to keep a copy.

8. No guarantees

If we are found responsible anyway, the most we will owe is what you paid us to use PainMap in the 12 months before the claim. For free users, that is $0. 4. Ce que fait PainMap

PainMap vous aide à indiquer où vous avez mal sur un corps en 3D, à répondre à des questions sur votre douleur, à tenir un journal et à créer un rapport pour un professionnel de la santé. L’application affiche aussi :

  • Des causes possibles : des pistes générales qui correspondent à vos réponses, à discuter avec un médecin, une infirmière ou un physiothérapeute. Ce n’est pas un diagnostic; elles peuvent être fausses et la bonne réponse peut ne pas y figurer.

Ne commencez, ne modifiez et n’arrêtez aucun exercice ni traitement sans en parler à votre professionnel de la santé, et arrêtez si la douleur augmente.

  • Elle ne surveille pas vos entrées et n’avertit personne en cas de problème.
